# Production replay stage instrumentation — INCOMPLETE

The harness now optionally uses Python 3.12 `sys.monitoring` on the actual single-instrument `run_expert_strategy_backtest` function. It resolves source markers exactly, fails if the expected markers are absent or reordered, and produces non-overlapping stage intervals. The output is an instrumented mechanics experiment, not the requested historical benchmark or a complete production pipeline profile. A regression test checks decisions, expert stance signatures and a native fill against an uninstrumented replay.

The 720-bar flat fixture ran under a Linux ARM Docker VM with a two-CPU quota, a 4 GiB memory cap and a 4 GiB combined memory/swap cap; it has no fills or funding and is not economic evidence. Three fresh-process uninstrumented child wall times were 2.271, 0.636 and 0.644 s; three monitored times were 0.642, 0.657 and 0.632 s. The first baseline includes colder image/page caches. Monitored median is 0.642 s versus 0.644 s baseline; the observed difference is within the noise and does not prove zero overhead. No OOM was recorded. These are measurements on an Apple M3 Pro VM, not a representative inexpensive VPS.

`timings.csv` reconciles the median monitored run to the parent-observed child wall time, including child interpreter startup and teardown. It reports approximately 31.6% imports after the internal timer began, 18.0% engine initialization and 29.9% `engine.run` inclusive of strategy callbacks. The remaining 16.4% is grouped interpreter startup, output and teardown; it cannot be separated from these measurements. The parent measurement file's own write is excluded. Other instrumentation/setup is explicit. These percentages describe this synthetic instrumented run only and must not be treated as costs of historical replay. `stage_stats.csv` gives the three monitored invocation samples, with nearest-rank p95 equal to the maximum for just three samples; that is not a stable tail estimate.

`memory.csv` shows RSS at stage boundaries for the median monitored run. Differences in RSS are not allocations attributable to a phase and can include allocator retention, native libraries and shared pages. `ru_maxrss` in the parent files remains the process high-water mark, not a per-phase peak. Monitoring snapshots read `/proc/self/statm` and their cost is included in the adjacent phase. The engine-run stage is inclusive: Python strategy and experts are nested within it, not additional elapsed time to add. Native time remains unresolved.

`breakout.json` is a separate 100-bar instrumented fixture, fixed to allow a bracket and two synthetic fills. Its source and settings are in the harness.

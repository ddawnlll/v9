## Contract

- One `BASELINE` and at most one arm per intervention (`MARKET_BELIEF`, `EXPERT_ROUTING`,
  `ENTRY_TIMING`, `POSITION_THESIS`, `PORTFOLIO_ALLOCATION`).
- A run is `MEASURED` only when it has trades, fills, gross/net PnL, net-R, drawdown, fees,
  funding and a deterministic percentile-bootstrap net-PnL interval, **and** fee/funding/
  slippage provenance is `OBSERVED` or `MODELED`, and microstructure is not `UNSUPPORTED`.
- `NOT_RUN`/`BLOCKED` arms carry **no numbers**; they must name their reasons.
- All measured arms must share dataset, config and native engine identity.
- Missing provenance blocks the whole report; no deltas are emitted.
- The only claim available is `DESCRIPTIVE_DELTA_ONLY`; there is no "superior" or
  profitability API. Delta confidence intervals use the conservative
  `[intervention_low − baseline_high, intervention_high − baseline_low]` bound.

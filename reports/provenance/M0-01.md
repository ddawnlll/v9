## Semantics (fail-closed)

- A dataset is `AVAILABLE` (hash + size) or `BLOCKED` (reason, no identity). There is no third state.
- `verify_manifest` returns `DRIFT` on hash mismatch, `BLOCKED` when authorized evidence is absent,
  and `PASS` only when every pinned artifact is present and matches. Missing evidence can never
  produce `PASS`.
- The manifest body is sealed by `manifest_digest`; a tampered body fails pydantic validation.
- `V9FeatureFlags` defaults all adaptive/order capabilities to `False`; `assert_safe_defaults`
  rejects an enabled flag and the manifest validator refuses to serialize one.

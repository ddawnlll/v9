## Three separated questions

1. **Boundary attribution.** `expected_amount = -quantity_at_boundary × causal_mark × rate`.
   Position evidence must be available at the boundary; a mark whose event or availability
   time is later is never used.
2. **Receipt finality.** `delayed = received_at > boundary`. If a settlement is received
   after the accounting cut it is claimed under, the report is `BLOCKED`
   (`NOT_FINAL_AT_ACCOUNTING_TIME`) even when the arithmetic agrees.
3. **Equity identity.**
   `final = initial + realized_cashflows + funding + (qty_final×mark_final − qty_initial×mark_opening)`.
   A supplied final equity that disagrees is a hard `EQUITY_MISMATCH`.

## Fail-closed classes

`DUPLICATE_SETTLEMENT`, `DUPLICATE_BOUNDARY_CLAIM`, `MISSING_EXPOSURE`, `NO_CAUSAL_MARK`,
`NOT_FINAL_AT_ACCOUNTING_TIME`, `FUNDING_MISMATCH`, `MISSING_FINAL_MARK`, `EQUITY_MISMATCH`,
`INVALID_RECORD`.

Unknown exposure and a missing causal mark produce `BLOCKED`, never zero funding.
Look-ahead position evidence (available after the boundary) is excluded, which turns the
attribution into `MISSING_EXPOSURE` rather than a guessed number.

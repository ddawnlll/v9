# M3-03 — HOLD / REDUCE / EXIT advisories and protective-intent proposals

Objective: turn the M3-02 recurrent thesis evaluation into typed, reproducible
management advisories (`HOLD`, `REDUCE`, `EXIT`) plus *separate* protective-intent
proposals, without granting any order authority, any autonomy over native stops, or
any permission that the native risk authority owns. Fail closed everywhere: unknown,
stale, refused or absent protection may never be reported as protected and may never
produce a silent `HOLD`.

Branch: `wave3/w3c` (base `integration/wave1`, `a6b5697`).

## Implementation

New module `src/v8_next/economics/management.py` (no edits to any pre-existing module,
test or doc).

- `ProtectionState` (`PROTECTED | UNPROTECTED | UNKNOWN | REJECTED | GAP`) is the only
  protection vocabulary. `_read_protection` normalises it and returns `(state, protected)`
  where `protected` is `True` **only** for a `PROTECTED` reading that also carries a stop
  price still on the safe side of the mark. A self-contradictory `PROTECTED` claim
  (missing stop, or stop at/through the mark) is downgraded to `UNKNOWN` before it can
  reach a consumer, so `ManagementDecision.protected` can never be `True` while the
  reading is unreadable.
- `PositionAdvisory` — frozen; `action` restricted to `HOLD`/`REDUCE`/`EXIT` by a field
  validator; `fraction` ∈ [0, 1] and non-zero only for `REDUCE`; `reduce_quantity` is the
  lot-rounded absolute size the fraction was derived from; `reason_codes` are unique,
  sorted by `ReasonCode` declaration order; `thesis_status` is carried through;
  `advisory_only: Literal[True]`.
- `ProtectiveIntent` (typed protective intent, reduce-only) + `ProtectiveProposal`
  (`kind`, `intent`, `quantity`, `price`, `reason`, `requires_risk_permission:
  Literal[True]`). Identity discipline copies the entry module: the intent id is the
  digest of the whole record minus the id, so every defining field contributes; proposal
  and intent must agree on position, quantity and trigger price.
- `ManagementDecision` — the envelope. Model-level invariants enforce the safety
  contract: `protected` ⇒ state is `PROTECTED`; `fail_closed` ⇒ action is `EXIT` (never a
  fail-closed `HOLD`); `admission_ok` ⇒ protected, not fail-closed and the action is in
  the legal set.
- `evaluate_management(evaluation, *, protection_state, fee_bps, mark_price, side,
  entry_price, quantity, initial_stop_price, instrument_id, protective_stop_price=None,
  lot_step, tick_size, venue_status=UNKNOWN, position_presence=UNCERTAIN, stale=True,
  armed_opportunity=False, competing_actions=())` — one pure call, no state, no clock
  beyond the evaluation's own `evaluated_ns`.

### Decision rules (documented, table-driven)

1. Thesis action = risk-first resolution of the evaluation's proposals restricted to
   `{HOLD, REDUCE, EXIT}` (`v8_next.economics.legality.resolve_conflict`, precedence
   `EXIT > REDUCE > ABSTAIN > WAIT > HOLD > ENTER`). A thesis that can only say `WAIT`
   yields no management action ⇒ fail closed to `EXIT`.
2. Any non-`PROTECTED` (or downgraded) protection reading forces `EXIT` and sets
   `fail_closed`, whatever the thesis says.
3. Competing candidates from other planes are resolved through the same domain
   precedence and can only escalate; anything outside the management vocabulary is
   ignored, so a competing `ENTER` can never become an advisory.
4. `REDUCE` is planned by `plan_partial_reduction`: `|quantity| * 0.5` floored onto the
   lot grid (never up), rejected if it floors to zero or would close the position — in
   that case the advisory escalates to `EXIT` (the only lot-valid way to cut risk).
   `fraction = reduce_quantity / |quantity|` quantised `ROUND_FLOOR` to 1e-9, so
   `fraction * quantity <= quantity` always.
5. `ThesisReason` → `ReasonCode` is a total projection (`_THESIS_REASON_CODE`, import
   guard pins totality), plus the protection-state codes and the action's own code.
   Emitted tuples are de-duplicated and sorted by declaration order, so output never
   depends on input order.
6. Protective proposals:
   - `PROTECTIVE_ADD` when not protected and the thesis' original stop is still
     protective relative to the mark. The price is always the *original* thesis stop —
     never a mark-derived or wider level — and no proposal is made once that stop has
     been passed.
   - `PROTECTIVE_BREAK_EVEN` when protected and the advisory is `HOLD`/`REDUCE`, only
     if the fee-inclusive break-even level tightens the current stop (LONG up, SHORT
     down; a level at or inside the current stop is refused as widening) and is still on
     the safe side of the mark. Suppressed on `EXIT`.
   - Break-even includes both fee legs: `E*(1+r)/(1-r)` long, `E*(1-r)/(1+r)` short with
     `r = fee_bps/10_000`, snapped *away* from the entry (ceiling long, floor short) onto
     the tick so the level always covers `r*(E+P)`.
7. `admission_ok = not closed ∧ not fail_closed ∧ protected ∧ action legal ∧
   evaluation.admission_ok`. It is a *forwarding* permission for the advisory, not an
   order permission: every proposal still carries `requires_risk_permission=True`.
8. `CLOSED` positions produce a `HOLD` placeholder that is never admitted (the advisory
   vocabulary has no no-op verb) with `POSITION_NONE` and no proposals.

### Deviations from the ticket wording (deliberate, fail-closed)

- `ProtectiveProposal.intent` is the ticket's permitted "typed protective intent"
  (`ProtectiveIntent`) rather than `EntryIntent`: an entry intent names an opportunity,
  an entry mode and an entry band, none of which describe a stop, and fabricating an
  `opportunity_id` for a stop would put a false identity on the replay surface.
  Reduce-only authority is expressed as `reduce_only: Literal[True]`, mirroring the
  native invariant without importing any adapter.
- `ProtectiveProposal.price` is a required `Decimal` (the ticket allowed `Decimal |
  None`): a proposal without a trigger price is not a proposal, so `None` is rejected at
  construction instead of becoming an unpriced, silently actionable record.

## Residual risks / limits

- **Not wired into any runtime path.** No caller constructs a `ManagementDecision` yet;
  the M3 integration wave must decide where `protection_state` and the fee/lot/tick
  inputs come from (native bracket report, instrument definition, fee schedule). Until
  then this is a contract plus proofs, not live behaviour.
- **Reason vocabulary is a projection.** `ReasonCode` is closed, so `ThesisReason`
  values are projected onto the closest decision codes (`REGIME_CHANGED` →
  `EVIDENCE_CONFLICT`, `EXPERT_CONTRADICTION`/`TIME_LIMIT` → `POSITION_REDUCE_REQUIRED`,
  `BELIEF_ABSTAIN` → `BELIEF_MISSING`, `EVIDENCE_INVALID` → `EVIDENCE_CONFLICT`). The
  losing granularity stays available on the evaluation itself; renaming any projection
  later is a replay-surface change. `POSITION_OPEN` accompanies fail-closed exits when
  the thesis was intact — it is a fact about the position, not an instruction, and the
  action code (`POSITION_EXIT_REQUIRED`) always wins in the same tuple.
- **Break-even triggers only on a coherent reading.** A `PROTECTED` reading whose stop
  is missing or already through the mark is downgraded to `UNKNOWN` and forced to
  `EXIT`; a stop at exactly the mark therefore fails closed rather than being treated as
  protective.
- **`admission_ok` is a forwarding flag, not an execution grant.** Even an admitted
  advisory requires the native risk authority; nothing here sizes, prices, submits or
  cancels an order, and no adapter is importable from the module (asserted by AST test).
- **Caller-config errors raise.** Negative/non-finite amounts, `fee_bps >= 10_000`,
  non-positive lot/tick and a non-`Decimal` quantity are programming errors and raise
  rather than degrading to a typed state. Only *market/protection data* degrades.
- **Rounding is venue-model-free.** Fee coverage assumes a simple bps-of-notional
  round-trip fee and no funding/slippage term; funding is out of scope here and stays
  with the accounting plane.
- BLOCKED items: none.

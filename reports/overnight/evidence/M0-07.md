## Objective

Stop intrabar/future data and incomplete protective evidence from being presented as
realized proof:

1. MFE/MAE may only accumulate from bars strictly after the entry eligibility boundary;
   mutating the entry bar's future high/low must not change any pre-entry information or
   the measured excursion.
2. STOP-FIRST is a declared model label, not an observation. A bar covering both stop and
   target must be recorded as an unresolved tie and the audit must report
   `NOT_IDENTIFIABLE`, never `PASS`.
3. Mechanical, economic, PIT, and audit-completeness verdicts are separate dimensions.
   A completeness `PASS` (label `AUDIT_COMPLETE`) is refused, with a typed error, when any
   required source ID is missing or a dimension has not run.

## What was implemented

### `src/v8_next/audit/trade_metrics.py` (extended, not rewritten)

- New pure helpers:
  - `entry_eligibility_boundary(opened_ns, *, intrabar_exclusion_ns=INTRABAR_EXCLUSION_NS)`
  - `post_entry_window(candles, *, opened_ns, exit_ns=None, intrabar_exclusion_ns=0)`
    returns only bars whose start is at/after the boundary, stops at the exit bar, and
    excludes any bar whose start cannot be established (fail closed). A bar that contains a
    mid-bar entry timestamp is therefore excluded.
  - `POST_ENTRY_ELIGIBILITY` documents the rule; `INTRABAR_EXCLUSION_NS = 0` records the
    bar-close replay assumption.
- `stop_first_path` now iterates `post_entry_window` and additionally reports
  `eligibility_boundary_ns`, `same_bar_stop_and_target`, and `tie_resolution`
  (`"STOP_FIRST_DECLARED"` when the tape hits both levels in one bar). STOP_FIRST outcomes
  and MAE capping are unchanged for unambiguous bars.
- `compute_trade_metrics`:
  - no-stop MFE/MAE now use `post_entry_window` instead of the raw position window (this
    was the remaining look-ahead path: the entry bar's high/low could inflate MFE/MAE when
    no stop existed);
  - a no-stop campaign with no post-entry bar yields `None` + `NO_POST_ENTRY_BAR` instead
    of borrowing entry-bar extrema;
  - new row fields: `entry_eligibility_rule`, `entry_eligibility_boundary_ns`,
    `entry_bars_excluded`, `same_bar_stop_and_target`, `tie_resolution`.
  - Fully-evidenced outputs are unchanged: `mfe_price`, `mae_price`, `mfe_r`, `mae_r`,
    `realized_gross_r`, `realized_net_r`, `measured_window_bars`, `holding_bars` all keep
    the pre-change values (exercised by `tests/test_audit_native.py`, see below).

### `src/v8_next/audit/verdicts.py` (new)

- `VerdictState` (`PASS | FAIL | NOT_IDENTIFIABLE | INVALID | INCOMPLETE | NOT_RUN | BLOCKED`).
- `AuditVerdict` (pydantic v2, frozen, `extra="forbid"`): independently-set `mechanical`,
  `economic`, `pit`, `completeness`, plus `reasons`, `labels`, `source_ids`.
  Construction raises `AuditVerdictError` (typed, `.code`) when `completeness=PASS` while
  `REQUIRED_SOURCE_IDS` (`position_id`, `campaign_id`, `opportunity_id`, `decision_id`) are
  missing, a dimension is `NOT_RUN/BLOCKED/INCOMPLETE/INVALID`, or a `MISSING_SOURCE_ID`
  reason is present.
- `derive_verdict(...)` computes completeness fail-closed:
  `BLOCKED > NOT_RUN > missing source IDs (INCOMPLETE) > INVALID > INCOMPLETE > PASS`.
- `verdict_from_trade_metrics(...)` derives mechanical/economic from a metrics row and
  defaults PIT to `NOT_RUN` (an explicit lineage audit is required for completion):
  - mechanical: `INVALID` for missing entry/quantity/time or zero stop distance;
    `NOT_IDENTIFIABLE` for `ENTRY_BAR_ONLY`, unresolved same-bar tie (label `STOP_FIRST`),
    missing protective stop, or unmeasured excursion; else `PASS`.
  - economic: `INCOMPLETE` without a realized outcome or without native protective-order
    evidence; `INVALID` with an invalid risk denominator; else `PASS`.
- `audit_label(verdict)` emits `AUDIT_COMPLETE` only for completeness `PASS`;
  `require_audit_complete(verdict)` raises `AuditVerdictError("AUDIT_NOT_COMPLETE")`.

## Residual risks

- MFE/MAE remains a bar-level measurement. STOP_FIRST is still a declared assumption for
  bars that touch a level; the new `same_bar_stop_and_target` field makes the ambiguity
  visible rather than resolving it, and the verdict dimension refuses to call such a path
  identified.
- `post_entry_window` uses `start_ns` when the tape provides it and the previous bar's end
  otherwise. A tape with gaps and no `start_ns` can under-include bars (fail-closed, not
  look-ahead) — conservative by design.
- `INTRABAR_EXCLUSION_NS` is 0 because native fills of the bar-close replay are stamped at
  the bar end. A future intra-bar execution stream must pass a non-zero exclusion or the
  containing bar is excluded by the `start_ns` rule.
- `economic` PASS means "realized, net, native-protective-order-evidenced"; it makes no
  claim that the strategy is profitable or that the path model is correct.
- `AuditVerdict.model_validate` routes mappings through the constructor, so the typed
  refusal applies there too; non-mapping model validation of an already-validated instance
  is unaffected.
- No live-trading or economic claims are made or tested.

# M2-06 — V8 policy-off parity and V9 shadow replay consistency

Objective: prove, with an independent differential harness, that the V8 baseline is unchanged with
V9 disabled and that V9 shadow decisions stay deterministic and side-effect-free (issue #21). The
harness locks an event fixture's opportunity selection, native orders, stop/protection orders,
fills, funding rows and audit-lineage verdict as canonical digests; a one-tick price change or a
one-cent fee change must fail, naming the stream.

## What was implemented

`src/v8_next/evaluation/shadow_parity.py` (new module; `DIGEST_VERSION = "v9.shadow-parity.1"`):

- **Typed fixture, no engine.** `ReplayScript` holds `BarEvent`, `OpportunityEvent`,
  `NativeDecisionEvent`, `NativeOrderEvent`, `ProtectionOrderEvent`, `FillEvent`,
  `CashflowEvent`, `FundingRowEvent` and `DecisionCutEvent` tuples. Money/price fields are
  `Decimal`-only (`BeforeValidator` rejects `float`/`str`, non-finite values). Duplicate ids per
  layer and duplicate `decision_time_ns` across cuts are refused at construction.
- **`ReplaySignature`** (frozen, `extra="forbid"`): one digest per native stream — `opportunity`,
  `orders`, `protection`, `fills`, `funding`, `lineage` — plus `policy_state: Literal["OFF",
  "SHADOW"]`, the adaptive `decisions` digest and the enumerated `decision_ids`. The validator
  refuses an OFF signature that carries a decision stream and a SHADOW signature that does not.
- **`capture_replay(events, *, policy_state)`.** OFF hashes the fixture only. SHADOW hashes the
  native streams, runs the adaptive chain per cut, then hashes the native streams again and raises
  `ShadowMutationError` if any digest moved. A SHADOW script with no cuts raises
  `ShadowNotExercised` (an empty run is not evidence); a fixture whose own lineage is not
  `COMPLETE` raises `LineageNotCertifiable`.
- **Adaptive chain.** `advance(previous_belief, view, context, now_ns=cut)` over PIT bar
  observations (only bars whose `end_ns <= decision_time_ns`), assembled into an M15
  `HorizonContext`. Evidence is derived from the belief by two declared witnesses: the structure
  direction at `STRUCTURE_WEIGHT = 0.40` and the named regime at its own score (quantised to
  `0.0001`). The belief's `UncertaintyState` maps to a declared ordinal band
  (`LOW 0.10 / MEDIUM 0.40 / HIGH 0.75 / UNKNOWN 1.00`) because the policy asks for a `Decimal`,
  not a probability. Opportunity lifecycle is read PIT from the fixture. Each cut then goes through
  `TraderDecisionPolicy.evaluate` and `DecisionLedger.append` with the belief's own
  `input_observation_ids` and their availability clocks; the ledger rejects non-PIT or
  unverifiable receipts, and no order-type or execution adapter is imported.
- **`compare(baseline, candidate) -> ParityVerdict`.** Reuses `GateStatus`, `StreamVerdict` and
  `ParityGateResult` from `v8_next.evaluation.resource_baseline`. Precedence mirrors
  `parity_gate`: any observed drift is `FAIL`, otherwise a missing digest is `BLOCKED`, and only a
  policy-off reference compared with a policy-off candidate whose six native streams all match is
  `PASS`. A shadow candidate is `BLOCKED` (not silently passed) and is certified by
  `assert_shadow_unmutated`, which requires reference OFF, candidate SHADOW, a populated decision
  stream and six identical native digests.
- **`pin` / `assert_pinned`.** `pin` emits `canonical()` JSON (sorted keys, no whitespace,
  `allow_nan=False`). `assert_pinned` round-trips text, mapping or model and raises
  `PinnedReferenceDrift` naming every drifted stream (`PINNED_DRIFT:<stream>`), plus typed refusals
  for a malformed reference and for a shadow signature. `_amount()` normalises `Decimal` numeric
  identity, so `1.0`/`1.00` are one amount while `1.00`/`1.01` are two.
- **Lineage.** `certify` runs over the fixture's five explicit layers at the script's declared
  `as_of_ns`; the lineage digest binds the verdict, the provenance digest, the join count, the
  campaigns and any violation codes. Cashflow amounts enter through the provenance digest (they
  have no stream of their own; tested explicitly).

## Order sensitivity (stated)

Native streams are content-addressed: each row list is sorted by a natural key
(`(submitted_ns, client_order_id)` for orders/protection, `(event_time_ns, fill_id)` for fills,
`(event_time_ns, funding_id)` for funding, `(selected_ns, opportunity_id)` for opportunities), so
reversing every stream of the fixture leaves all native digests — and the pin — unchanged. The
decision stream is order-sensitive by construction (belief update is stateful via
`advance(previous, ...)`), which is why cuts must have distinct `decision_time_ns` and are replayed
in time order; duplicate cut instants are refused rather than ordered arbitrarily.

## Tests

`tests/test_shadow_parity.py` — 29 tests, all passing:

- Policy-off signature is complete (six native digests) and carries no adaptive stream.
- Policy-off replay reproduces the golden `PINNED_REFERENCE_JSON` byte for byte, is stable across a
  JSON round trip, and is accepted by `assert_pinned` as text, dict and model.
- Policy-on shadow over the same fixture: `assert_shadow_unmutated` passes, all six native digests
  equal the policy-off capture, and the decision stream is populated with exactly four ids (one per
  cut) that are distinct, ordered and equal to `shadow_decisions()` receipts.
- Shadow receipts are verified, `advisory_only`, policy v1, and each action lies inside
  `LegalActionTable.legal` for its cut (position, venue, staleness, arming); the adaptive chain
  produced `{ABSTAIN, ENTER, HOLD}` and the PIT cut before the opportunity was released carries
  `OPPORTUNITY_NOT_ARMED` while the venue-unknown cut carries `VENUE_UNKNOWN`.
- Simulated drift: one cent on an order price / protection trigger / fill fee / funding amount /
  cashflow amount, or a changed opportunity direction, fails `compare` with `DIGEST_DRIFT:<stream>`
  for exactly that stream; all other streams stay `PASS`.
- Fail-closed: every native digest removed in turn yields `BLOCKED`
  (`DIGEST_MISSING:<stream>:candidate|baseline`); a shadow candidate and a shadow reference are
  `BLOCKED`; `assert_shadow_unmutated` refuses OFF/OFF, SHADOW/SHADOW, an empty decision stream and
  a mutated stream; unknown policy state, non-`ReplayScript` input and a foreign cut instrument are
  refused; an incomplete or late lineage fixture cannot be captured.
- Pin refusals: malformed JSON, non-object JSON, echo of a shadow pin, and a drifted signature
  (`PINNED_DRIFT:orders`).
- Reordered fixture: native pin unchanged and decision ids identical; duplicate cut instants
  refused.
- Equivalence: `0.0595` and `0.05950` produce the same digest.
- Structural impossibility: AST scan shows no `nautilus_trader`, `v8_next.adapters`,
  `v8_next.exchange`, `v8_next.execution` or `v8_next.venues` import and no order-primitive name
  (`ClientOrderId`, `OrderFactory`, `OrderType`, `OrderSide`, `OrderList`, submit/place/cancel/
  modify verbs); `ReplaySignature` has no order-shaped field.

## Residual risks / limits

- The fixture is synthetic and deterministic by design: the harness proves digest-level invariance
  and decision determinism, not that a Nautilus run over a real tape is unchanged. A tape-bound
  policy-off replay would need a separate run bound to `TapeIdentity` / `parity_gate`.
- `compare` deliberately reports `BLOCKED` for a SHADOW candidate instead of `PASS`: certifying
  baseline parity and proving shadow non-mutation are different claims, and merging them would let
  a shadow run be published as a baseline result. Non-mutation has its own gate.
- The uncertainty bands and `STRUCTURE_WEIGHT` are declared constants, not fitted: they shape the
  advisory decision stream only and cannot change native evidence. Changing them changes the
  decision digest (the golden covers OFF only; shadow has no pin).
- The golden is bound to `DIGEST_VERSION`/canonicalisation. Any deliberate payload change must be a
  new version plus an explicit re-pin; there is no auto-regeneration path.
- Cashflow amounts are covered only through the lineage provenance digest (tested); adding a
  dedicated stream would change the pinned reference.
- Shadow decisions are advisory receipts: no economic, fill, cashflow or live-trading claim is made
  and no order object exists in this module. BLOCKED items: none.

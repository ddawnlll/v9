# M4-02 (#30) — Costed native finalist replay and single-intervention ablations

**Branch:** `wave5/m4-02`
**Status:** `PARTIAL` — baseline measured and costed; all five V9 intervention arms are
`NOT_RUN` by design (adaptive order side effects are disabled), so no intervention delta is
published.

## What was built

* `src/v8_next/evaluation/ablations.py`
  * Frozen models: `Intervention` (`BELIEF | EXPERT_ROUTING | ENTRY_TIMING |
    POSITION_THESIS | ALLOCATION`), `Provenance`, `ArmResult`, `ArmDelta`, `AblationReport`.
  * `run_native_baseline(...)` drives the **existing** native path
    (`adapters/portfolio_backtest.run_portfolio_backtest` over the V8 28-expert ensemble).
  * `run_ablation(...)` replays the baseline and assembles the five intervention arms.
    Every intervention without a caller-supplied, already-produced **costed** `ArmResult`
    is `NOT_RUN` with the reason `ADAPTIVE_ORDERS_DISABLED_SHADOW_ONLY`.
  * `build_report(...)` refuses to compute a delta unless both arms are costed, and
    `AblationReport.__post_init__` refuses `status: COMPLETE` while any intervention is
    `NOT_RUN`, lacks a net-R delta, or lacks a delta CI.
  * CLI: `python -m v8_next.evaluation.ablations --tape <path> --out <json>`
    (optional `--supplied-arm INTERVENTION=PATH`, `--no-baseline`, profile/fee knobs).
* `tests/test_ablations.py` — 17 tests: hand-computed delta arithmetic, CI presence,
  NOT_RUN produces no delta, COMPLETE gate, provenance carry-through, UNSUPPORTED-not-zero,
  deterministic JSON, bootstrap determinism, mismatch/uncosted supplied arms, and one real
  native replay over the authorised tape.

## Cost model / provenance

`fee_total` is summed from the engine's order-fill commission column. `funding_total` is
**isolated by a dual replay**: the same strategy over the same bars once with the tape's
funding rows and once without; the balance difference is the funding PnL contribution, and
the two replays must share an identical trade signature (`DUAL_RUN_ISOLATION=VALID`) or
funding is published as `None` with a named unsupported reason. `net_pnl` is the native
account balance delta (commissions and funding are already inside `position.realized_pnl`).
`net_r` is the mean per-campaign net R (campaign PnL ÷ bracket risk = entry notional ×
`bracket_stop_pct`); the CI is a seeded percentile bootstrap (2000 resamples, seed
20251105). `max_drawdown` is peak-to-trough of the realized-equity curve; the open campaign
is counted, never marked into the realized curve.

Each of the following is published as a basis string on the arm, not assumed:
fee model and rates, funding coverage/mark basis, intrabar tie basis
(`NAUTILUS_2.0.0rc4_BAR_EXECUTION_DEFAULT`, no STOP_FIRST claim), slippage basis
(`fill_model=default`, `prob_slippage=0.0`), and partial-fill basis
(`ORDER_FILL_REPORT_ROWS=89 PARTIAL_ORDERS=0`).

  baseline arm.

  number or delta.
* Single instrument, hourly bars, one authorised tape (2025 Q1). No multi-asset or
  out-of-sample claim is made here.

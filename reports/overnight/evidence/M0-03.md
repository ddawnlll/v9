# M0-03 — Opportunity-to-cashflow lineage with fail-closed joins

**Issue:** #4 · **Worker:** W3 (`AuditOracle`) · **Branch:** `wave1/w3`
**Base commit:** `0b69fc45c197354600256963249ca7b1f19b6bbd` (main, read-only reference)

## Objective

Carry stable event-time and native ids across decisions → orders → fills → cashflows;
validate id uniqueness and campaign membership; never substitute a last-seen campaign;
and produce typed failures for mislinked, orphaned, duplicate and late records so that
no independent report can certify `COMPLETE` while any join is missing.

## What was implemented

Extended `src/v8_next/audit/lineage.py` (existing `LineageAuditor`, `LineageAuditReport`
and `PointInTimeLeakage` are untouched and still exported):

- Frozen `slots=True` records: `LineageDecision`, `LineageOrder`, `LineageFill`,
  `LineageCashflow`, bundled as `LineageChain` with an order-independent canonical
  `digest()` (reuses `telemetry.identity.canonical_digest`).
- `certify(chain, *, as_of_ns) -> LineageCertification`: a pure, non-raising verifier that
  returns `COMPLETE` / `INCOMPLETE` / `BLOCKED`, the typed violations, `joins_checked`,
  the campaign ids **as declared by the decisions**, and the provenance digest.
- `require_complete(certification)` raises the first typed failure; a non-`COMPLETE`
  verdict can never be consumed as proof.
- Typed failures (`LineageViolation` subclasses carrying a `LineageViolationCode`):
  `MissingJoin`, `OrphanRecord`, `DuplicateFill`, `DuplicateRecord`, `LateRecord`,
  `CampaignMismatch`, `MislinkedRecord`, `EventTimeInversion`.
- Verdict rules:
  - `COMPLETE` requires all four layers to hold evidence, every join to resolve, unique
    ids per layer, campaign ids agreeing with the parent, no event-time inversion, and no
    record whose event or availability time exceeds `as_of_ns`.
  - `INCOMPLETE` for missing/orphaned/duplicated/mismatched/inverted records.
  - `BLOCKED` when a layer is structurally empty or any record is late (uncertifiable
    evidence), including the empty chain and chains whose decision layer is absent.
- Terminal coverage: every fill must be referenced by at least one cashflow; otherwise a
  `MissingJoin` is emitted for that fill.

## Residual risks / limits

- `certify` validates joins and identity, not money: cashflow `amount` is carried and
  digested but conservation is `audit.cashflow`'s job.
- One cashflow per fill is not required, but at least one is: a fill with no cashflow is
  a missing join by contract. A venue that legitimately produces zero cash for a fill
  must emit an explicit zero-amount row.
- Campaign membership is checked edge-by-edge against the declared parent; an internally
  consistent relabel of a whole branch is caught only when it contradicts the instrument
  or another edge (same limit as the M0-02 oracle).
- Record construction validates types only; blank ids are *reported* as `MissingJoin`,
  never silently defaulted.

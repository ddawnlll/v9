# M2-04 — Unified recurrent `TraderDecisionPolicy` in shadow mode

Objective: replace the single entry-veto gate with one context-aware advisory decision
(issue #19) that consumes belief, expert stances, the opportunity lifecycle, position presence,
venue state and portfolio capacity, and emits exactly one of the six typed actions
(`WAIT`/`ENTER`/`HOLD`/`REDUCE`/`EXIT`/`ABSTAIN`). The policy is shadow-only: its only output is a
`TraderDecision` with `advisory_only=True`.

## What was implemented

`src/v8_next/economics/policy.py` (new module):

- `DecisionRequest` — frozen dataclass envelope carrying `decision_time_ns`, `instrument_id`,
  `venue_status`, `position: PositionState`, `portfolio: PortfolioState`, `opportunity`,
  `belief`, `expert_stances`. The envelope validates itself (malformed envelope is a caller bug and
  raises); unreadable *data* inside it degrades to a typed fail-closed action.
- Duck-typed views via `typing.Protocol`: `BeliefView` (`version_hash`, `regime`, `uncertainty`,
  `stale`, `abstain`), `OpportunityView` (`opportunity_id`, `lifecycle`), `ExpertStanceView`
  (`expert_id`, `stance`, `weight`). `v8_next.belief` is **not** imported (built concurrently);
  both sides only need to satisfy the structural shape. `as_of_ns` is honoured when present on a
  belief/opportunity view (PIT enforcement), and no default is invented when absent.
- `PositionState` (presence + signed quantity + absolute exposure) and `PortfolioState`
  (equity, committed exposure, `CapacityState.AVAILABLE/FULL/UNKNOWN`, default `UNKNOWN`) are
  frozen pydantic models; money is `Decimal`-only via a `BeforeValidator` that rejects floats.
  Contradictory states (`NONE` with exposure, `OPEN` with nothing, `UNCERTAIN` with numbers) are
  rejected at construction.
- `TraderDecisionPolicy.evaluate(request) -> TraderDecision` — single decision entrypoint.
  `TraderDecisionPolicy.explain(request) -> PolicyExplanation` returns the full provenance
  (candidates, legal set, support/contradict weights, net ratio, uncertainty, regime, lifecycle,
  constraints) for the same cut; `shadow_mode` is a read-only property pinned `True`.
- `POLICY_VERSION = "trader-policy-v1"`, documented thresholds
  (`ENTER_MIN_SUPPORT=0.50`, `ENTER_MIN_NET_RATIO=0.25`, `REDUCE_NET_RATIO=-0.20`,
  `EXIT_NET_RATIO=-0.60`, `MIXED_EVIDENCE_RATIO=0.25`, `ENTRY_UNCERTAINTY_CEILING=0.60`,
  `MAX_GROSS_EXPOSURE_RATIO=0.80`) and `POLICY_HASH` = `canonical_digest` over
  version + thresholds + `PRECEDENCE`. `POLICY_HASHES` is the registry receipts certify against.

Decision derivation (deterministic, order-independent):

1. Normalise belief, opportunity and stances. Unreadable belief ⇒ `ABSTAIN` + `BELIEF_MISSING`;
   dated after the cut ⇒ `INPUT_FUTURE`; missing opportunity ⇒ `ABSTAIN` +
   `OPPORTUNITY_NOT_ARMED`; terminal lifecycle with exposure ⇒ `EXIT` candidate; unreadable stance,
   out-of-range weight or duplicated `expert_id` ⇒ `ABSTAIN` + `EVIDENCE_CONFLICT`.
2. Weighted support/contradiction → `net_ratio`; flat: entry requires absolute support, net
   direction, non-stale belief, armed opportunity, `AVAILABLE` capacity, positive equity and
   gross exposure inside budget, otherwise `WAIT`; exposed: contradiction above `EXIT` threshold
   ⇒ `EXIT`, above `REDUCE` ⇒ `REDUCE`, otherwise `HOLD` (`SCALE_IN_DISABLED` when support is
   strong, since scale-in is not enabled).
3. Gross exposure over `equity × 0.80` proposes `REDUCE` independently of the belief.
4. Candidates are intersected with `LegalActionTable.legal(...)` and resolved with
   `resolve_legal_conflict`, so venue `UNKNOWN`, `UNCERTAIN` position, stale inputs and unarmed
   opportunities cannot widen the set. Reason codes come from the existing `ReasonCode` enum —
   **no enum extension was needed and none was made.**

## Tests

`tests/test_trader_decision_policy.py` — 43 tests, all passing:

- All six actions reachable from documented fixtures (table-driven) plus a test that the union of
  the scenarios covers `set(DecisionAction)` exactly; each asserts action, intent kind, advisory
  flag, `decision_id` binding, reason code, legal membership and explanation agreement.
- Determinism: replay mints identical decision and explanation; all permutations of expert order
  mint one `decision_id`; reason codes canonical.
- Fail closed: stale belief never enters (even with weight 1.0); venue `UNKNOWN` allows only
  WAIT/ABSTAIN across four distinct scenarios; missing opportunity ⇒ `ABSTAIN`; unarmed
  opportunity ⇒ `WAIT`; `UNCERTAIN` position ⇒ `ABSTAIN`; missing/malformed belief ⇒ `ABSTAIN`;
  future-dated belief/opportunity ⇒ `INPUT_FUTURE`; unknown/full capacity blocks entry; high
  uncertainty blocks entry; conflicting panel never enters; unreadable stance and duplicate
  expert ids abstain; gross-exposure breach forces `REDUCE`; flat contradiction abstains.
- Explainability: `explain` reports weights, net ratio, regime, lifecycle, armed/stale and the
  blocking constraint token.
- Envelope/shape: negative time, blank instrument and float money are refused.
- No order path: AST scan of the module shows no `nautilus`/`adapter` import and no import of
  `v8_next.belief`; no order verb is called; `TraderDecision` has no quantity/price/side field and
  refuses `advisory_only=False`.

## Residual risks / limits

- The `regime` field is carried and explained but does not yet gate actions; regime-conditioned
  thresholds are a future policy version, not a hidden branch.
- Stale belief yields `ABSTAIN` for the whole cut (including management candidates inferred from
  evidence); only opportunity invalidation independently proposes `EXIT`. That is deliberate
  fail-closed behaviour, not an oversight.
- `PENDING_INTENT` with zero filled quantity is treated as flat-but-in-flight: `ENTER` requires
  `presence == NONE`, which is stricter than `LEGAL_ACTION_TABLE` (the table alone would allow a
  retry after `CANCELED`/`REJECTED`).
- `as_of_ns` enforcement depends on the caller-provided view exposing it; a view without it makes
  no PIT claim at this layer, and the receipt plane (M2-05) carries the observation-level proof.
- Thresholds are compiled constants, not yet calibrated; changing any of them requires a new
  `POLICY_VERSION` and `POLICY_HASH` (enforced by receipts certifying hashes via the registry).
- No economic P&L claim is made. BLOCKED items: none.

# M4-03 — Walk-forward, multiple-testing and sealed OOS protocol (#31)

Status: **IMPLEMENTED_WITH_EVIDENCE** (integration and issue closure remain the director's call).

## Objective

Enforce dataset roles, preregistered hypothesis budgets, purged walk-forward windows, repeated-
testing corrections and a sealed final evaluation whose read authority is granted separately.
Reusing sealed data, testing outside the declared family, or changing gates after the fact must
be detectable; `NO_EDGE` and `STOP` must be publishable conclusions rather than silent failures.

## What was implemented

`src/v8_next/evaluation/protocol.py` (new, 1015 lines)

- `DatasetRole` — `DEVELOPMENT | VALIDATION | SEALED_OOS`; `OPEN_ROLES` is
  `{DEVELOPMENT, VALIDATION}`; `ROLE_ORDER` is the canonical report order (all three,
  including unused roles).
- `AccessLedger` — append-only (`entries` is a tuple, no update/clear). `request_access(role,
  purpose, budget, *, slice_id, issued_ns, authorization)` returns an `AccessToken` only when
  the policy permits: open roles need a non-empty purpose and a positive budget; `SEALED_OOS`
  needs a `SealedAuthorization`. Token identity is
  `canonical_digest("research-access", …)` over seq/role/purpose/budget/slice/issued_ns/
  authorization_id, so identical reads reproduce identical tokens.
- `SealedAuthorization` — pydantic model whose private `capability` field must be the module's
  sole `_SEALED_MINT` object. Direct construction (forging field values) fails validation;
  only `authorize_sealed(granted_by, purpose, reference, max_slices, issued_ns)` mints one.
  Authorization identity includes the reference, so a genuine re-authorization is a distinct
  mint while repeating the identical grant is the same object.
- Reuse detection: a sealed read whose authorization already opened the same slice raises
  `SealedReuseViolation` (`code="SEALED_SLICE_REUSE"`, naming `slice_id` and
  `authorization_id`); one authorization may not open a second slice; an exhausted
  `max_slices` is `SealedAccessError`. `AccessLedger.from_entries` rehydrates a persisted
  ledger and re-proves the invariants, so a duplicated persisted read is refused.
  `reuse_detection()` reports, per sealed slice, `accesses`, the authorization ids and
  `reused`/`permitted` (permitted only when each read is backed by a distinct mint).
- `AccessReport` — `consumed_roles`, per-role `RoleSummary(accesses, budget_total, purposes,
  slices)` for every role (unused roles appear with zero), `sealed_slices`, and a
  `lineage_digest` over every recorded read.
- `HypothesisFamily(name, hypotheses)` — explicit declared family; `declared_size` is the
  family size. `multiple_testing(p_values, family, alpha, method)` refuses a tested set that
  differs from the declared family (`HypothesisBudgetViolation`,
  `code="HYPOTHESIS_BUDGET_VIOLATION"`), validates every p-value, and applies closed-form
  Bonferroni or Holm. `method=None` yields a `corrected=False` `TestingReport` whose
  `verdict_for()` resolves to `Verdict.INVALID` — an uncorrected claim is never `PASS`.
  `degrees_of_freedom` records the family size.
- `purged_walk_forward(total_bars, folds, purge_bars, embargo_bars, min_train_bars)` —
  partitions bars into `folds + 1` blocks (block 0 is initial training history) and, for every
  test block `[start, end)`, subtracts `[start - purge, start)` and `[end, end + embargo)`
  from every fold's training candidate. Training ranges are sorted disjoint half-open
  `IndexRange`s; a fold without `min_train_bars` is a typed `WalkForwardError`
  (`code="WALK_FORWARD_INVALID"`), never silently dropped.
- `Verdict` — `PASS | NO_EDGE | INCONCLUSIVE | STOP | INVALID`; `NEGATIVE_OUTCOMES` is
  `{NO_EDGE, STOP}`.
- `assess_verdict(preregistration, ledger, token, claimed, testing)` — the only path to
  `PASS`. Structural refusals raise `VerdictRefused`: unregistered token, token/record
  mismatch, role substitution, sealed read without recorded authorization, an outcome the
  registration never accepted, a family larger than the budget, a correction other than the
  registered one, or an incomplete preregistration (`PreregistrationError` from `validate()`).
  Uncorrected or unsupported claims resolve to `INVALID` (`UNCORRECTED_CLAIM`,
  `CLAIM_NOT_SUPPORTED`). `NO_EDGE`/`STOP` return as accepted outcomes
  (`ACCEPTED_NEGATIVE_OUTCOME`); `INCONCLUSIVE` is accepted (`ACCEPTED_OUTCOME`) and asserts
  nothing. Only a sealed-OOS `PASS` sets `primary_edge_eligible=True`;
  `require_primary_edge()` refuses everything else (`NOT_PRIMARY_OOS_EVIDENCE`), so a
  development/validation result can never be published as primary OOS edge.
- `build_result_report(...)` / `ResultReport` — registered role, verdict/code, primary-edge
  eligibility, `consumed_roles`, per-role summaries for all three roles, `lineage_digest` and
  `degrees_of_freedom`, so development, validation and sealed OOS are separately visible.

## Acceptance evidence

`tests/test_research_protocol.py` — 43 passed, 1 skipped (statsmodels cross-check).

- Open roles: development and validation tokens issued without authorization.
- Sealed refused without authorization (`SEALED_ACCESS_DENIED`, field `authorization`); a
  fabricated `SealedAuthorization` fails pydantic validation; a purpose mismatch is refused.
- Reuse: same authorization + same slice twice raises `SEALED_SLICE_REUSE`; one authorization
  cannot open two slices; a re-authorized read is permitted, recorded, and reported as
  `reused=True, permitted=True`; a duplicated persisted entry is refused on rehydration.
- Report: `consumed_roles == (DEVELOPMENT, VALIDATION, SEALED_OOS)` when all three are read;
  unused roles appear with zero accesses; `lineage_digest` changes when a read is recorded and
  is stable otherwise.
- Walk-forward: parametrised over four plans; asserted for every fold and every test block
  that no training index satisfies `test.start - purge <= index < test.start` or
  `test.end <= index < test.end + embargo`; deterministic across calls; purge/embargo
  measurably remove bars; impossible plans and purge-consuming plans are refused.
- Multiple testing: Bonferroni over a family of 5 turns p=0.03 from significant to
  non-significant (0.15); Holm is monotone and never below raw; family-size mismatch raises
  `HYPOTHESIS_BUDGET_VIOLATION`; uncorrected report yields `INVALID` even for p=0.001; a
  corrected claim outside the preregistration yields `INVALID`.
- Verdict gate: `NO_EDGE`/`STOP` accepted first-class; `PASS` without correction, without the
  registered hypothesis in the tested family (`HYPOTHESIS_NOT_TESTED`), or without that
  hypothesis surviving correction (`CLAIM_NOT_SUPPORTED`) is `INVALID`; `PASS` refuses an
  incomplete preregistration, a token the ledger never issued, a role substitution, and a
  family whose name is not the registered budget family (`FAMILY_MISMATCH`); `PASS` with a
  corrected family on a sealed role is `primary_edge_eligible`; a development `PASS` is not
  and `require_primary_edge` refuses it.
- The skipped test cross-checks our Bonferroni/Holm against `evaluation.multitest`
  (statsmodels) when that optional dependency is installed; statsmodels is **not installed**
  in this environment, so the cross-check is `SKIPPED`, not passed.

## Residual risks / NOT_RUN

- The family correction is implemented in closed form (Bonferroni/Holm) because
  `evaluation.multitest` delegates to statsmodels, which is not installed here; the optional
  cross-check test is written and skips without it. FDR methods are deliberately not offered.
- Hypothesis budget is enforced as `family_size <= max_hypotheses` at verdict time; per-trial
  spend against `max_trials` is recorded in the ledger budget but not decremented by an
  execution loop (**NOT_RUN**; there is no live evaluation loop in this slice).
- The ledger is in-process and rehydratable via `from_entries`; no SQLite persistence table
  was added (**NOT_RUN**).
- Wiring into `evaluation.runner` / `gate_resolution` / CLI is **NOT_RUN** (out of slice
  scope); no economic or live-trading claim is made.

## Objective

Build point-in-time 15m/1h/4h context from `ObservationView`s: no look-ahead when
the higher-timeframe bar is unfinished, explicit missing/late horizon state, and a
horizon schema independent of any strategy.

## Implemented (`src/v8_next/domain/horizons.py`)

- `Timeframe` — `M15 | H1 | H4` with `duration_ns` (15m, 1h, 4h).
- `HorizonAlignment` — frozen Pydantic model `timeframe, as_of_ns, complete, quality`.
  `as_of_ns` is the selected bar's close, or `0` when no bar is admissible;
  `complete` records whether the selected bar spans at least one full timeframe
  window.
- `HorizonContext` — frozen Pydantic model
  `instrument_id, decision_time_ns, alignments: dict[Timeframe, HorizonAlignment]`;
  the validator requires exactly one alignment per `Timeframe`, correctly keyed.
  `alignment(timeframe)` returns the (always present) alignment.
- `assemble_context(views, *, instrument_id, decision_time_ns)`:
  - every timeframe appears in the output; a timeframe absent from `views` is
    explicitly `as_of_ns=0, complete=False, quality=MISSING`;
  - admissibility is two-sided: `start_ns + timeframe.duration_ns <=
    decision_time_ns` **and** `end_ns <= decision_time_ns` **and**
    `available_ns <= decision_time_ns`; the decision time, not the view cut, is
    the gate;
  - selects the latest admissible bar by `(start_ns, end_ns)` — deterministic for
    aligned, off-grid and gapped inputs;
  - quality = `view.quality_at(instrument_id, decision_time_ns)` (so quality
    judgements made after the decision do not leak backwards) combined with
    `MISSING` when no bar is admissible, and `STALE` when the selected bar is
    partial or a further full window has elapsed since its close without newer
    data;
  - rejects unknown timeframe keys and non-integer/bool decision times.

## Acceptance evidence

- **Unfinished 1h/4h bars never appear in an earlier 15m decision** —
  `test_unfinished_higher_timeframe_bars_never_reach_an_earlier_decision` captures
  views at 17:00/21:00 and decides at 15:40: 15m aligns to 15:30, 1h to 15:00 and
  4h to 12:00; the bars closing at 15:45/16:00/16:00+ are excluded.
- **Late view cut is not a license to look ahead** —
  `test_late_availability_is_not_used_and_quality_does_not_leak_backwards`: a
  14:00-15:00 H1 bar first published at 16:30 is in a 17:00 view but is not
  admissible at 15:40; `quality_at(15:40)` is `OK` while the view's own worst
  quality is `CONTRADICTORY`, and the alignment is `STALE` (late publication),
  never `CONTRADICTORY`.
- **Missing horizons are explicit, never invented** —
  `test_absent_timeframes_are_explicitly_missing`: `H1`/`H4` are
  `as_of_ns=0, complete=False, quality=MISSING`; all three timeframes are always
  present.
- **Aligned / gapped / off-grid determinism** —
  `test_unfinished_higher_timeframe_bars_never_reach_an_earlier_decision` (aligned),
  `test_gapped_series_is_complete_but_stale` (gap → latest complete bar +
  `STALE`), `test_off_grid_bars_resolve_deterministically` (13:07 and 13:37 H1
  bars, reversed input order gives the same alignment, latest start wins),
  `test_partial_bar_is_incomplete_and_stale` (sub-timeframe bar →
  `complete=False`, `STALE`).
- **Worst quality propagates** — `test_worst_quality_propagates_into_the_alignment`
  (`STALE` bar → `STALE`; `MISSING` auxiliary observation → `MISSING`).
- **Immutability / later records cannot rewrite the context** —
  `test_context_is_immutable_and_survives_later_records`: appending a later
  observation and a new H4 view leaves the earlier context and the earlier view
  unchanged; frozen field assignment raises.
- **View cut at the decision** —
  `test_view_cut_at_the_decision_time_rejects_the_in_progress_bar`: the
  15:00-16:00 bar is `NOT_YET_AVAILABLE` at 15:40 and the context uses 14:00-15:00.
- **Schema/input fail-closed** —
  `test_context_schema_rejects_incomplete_or_mismatched_alignments` (missing or
  mis-keyed timeframes raise) and `test_invalid_inputs_fail_closed` (empty
  instrument, bool decision time, non-`Timeframe` key raise).

## Residual risks

- `as_of_ns = 0` is the explicit "no admissible bar" sentinel. It cannot collide
  with a real bar because observation event times are validated non-negative and a
  real bar close is > 0.
- `complete` describes the *selected* bar, not whether the most recent interval is
  present. A complete-but-old selection is reported through `quality=STALE`; a
  consumer that treats `complete=True` as "current" would be misreading the
  schema. The fail-closed signal is `quality`.
- Off-grid bars are trusted to be the timeframe the caller placed in the mapping;
  a shorter bar is downgraded (`complete=False`, `STALE`), while a longer bar that
  extends beyond the decision time is excluded by `end_ns <= decision_time_ns`.
- `HorizonContext.alignments` is a plain `dict` inside a frozen model: attribute
  assignment is blocked, but a caller could mutate the nested mapping. The
  library itself always builds a fresh dict per call.
- No strategy, signal or economic interpretation is encoded; the schema is
  strategy-independent by construction.

# M4-04 (#32) — PIT altcoin universe and independent market regime coverage

**Status: reporting machinery COMPLETE; altcoin re-evaluation NOT_RUN.**
`implementation_status`: the coverage report, integrity checks and CLI are implemented and
tested against synthetic tapes plus the real in-repo BTC tape. No altcoin tape exists in this
repository, so the universe is `NOT_RUN` with a named blocker, every regime conclusion is
`NOT_RUN`, and no economic claim is made. A witness that cannot be observed is reported
`BLOCKED`/`NOT_RUN`, never `PASS`.

## 1. Objective

Qualify a point-in-time altcoin universe beyond the BTC 2025-01..04 development slice: which
symbols are covered, over which periods, with which delistings, missing bars, and
funding/mark/metadata history; and whether independent (non-BTC) regimes can be evaluated with
explicit uncertainty rather than survivorship-biased or synthesized data.

## 2. Implementation

`src/v8_next/evaluation/coverage.py` (new):

* `SymbolCoverage` (frozen): symbol, dataset role (`DEVELOPMENT` / `VALIDATION` /
  `SEALED_OOS`, reusing `protocol.DatasetRole`), first/last bar open, `bar_count`,
  `expected_bars`, `missing_bar_ratio`, `funding_rows`, `expected_funding_rows`,
  `mark_rows`, `expected_mark_rows`, `metadata_versions`, `delisted_at`, `survivorship_risk`
  + named reason, `rows_after_as_of`, `source_sha256`, status and named `gaps`. A model
  validator recomputes every ratio from the counts and refuses an inconsistent row.
* `CoverageAggregate`: totals, per-role symbol counts, missing-bar ratio, funding/mark totals,
  delisted symbols, aggregate survivorship flag; counts and ratio are re-derived and checked.
* `CoverageReport`: per-symbol rows ordered by (role, symbol), aggregate coverage,
  `data_status` (`AVAILABLE` / `PARTIAL` / `BLOCKED`), `universe_status`
  (`PROVISIONED` / `NOT_RUN`), explicit `blockers`, and a `regimes` block. `to_json()` is
  canonical (sorted keys, fixed separators) and `digest()` is a domain-separated canonical
  digest, so identical inputs yield byte-identical documents.
* `build_coverage_report(tape_paths, *, expected_interval_ns, roles, as_of_ns=None,
  metadata=None, regime_outcomes=None)`:
  * streams `tape.jsonl`, converts venue milliseconds to nanoseconds, and counts kline,
    funding and mark channels separately;
  * computes `expected_bars` from the observed span and the caller's interval;
    every `BARS:gap:…` names the instant and the number of missing bars;
  * rejects duplicate timestamps (`DUPLICATE_TIMESTAMP`), out-of-order rows
    (`OUT_OF_ORDER_ROWS`) and off-grid bars (`NON_ALIGNED_TIMESTAMP`) with
    `TapeIntegrityError`; a ratio over ambiguous bars would be fabricated;
  * hashes the tape bytes (`sha256`) and publishes the manifest/record schema versions;
  * resolves instrument metadata at `as_of_ns` through the existing
    `v8_next.adapters.universe.resolve_metadata` (PIT: records readable later are not used),
    reports `delisted_at`, and marks bars at/after a resolved delisting `BLOCKED`;
  * sets `survivorship_risk` with a named reason whenever delisting history is absent or
    unresolvable, and reports an altcoin universe with no delisted symbol as
    `SURVIVORSHIP:NO_DELISTED_SYMBOL_IN_UNIVERSE`;
  * excludes rows after `as_of_ns` from every count and reports them as `rows_after_as_of`.
* `regimes`: a descriptive rolling-return/variance segmentation (24-bar window, +2%/−2%
  trend thresholds, 1%² per-bar variance for high-vol) reports pooled bar counts per regime
  across supplied symbols, plus warmup bars, the method, and an explicit uncertainty note.
  `RegimeConclusion` is `NO_EDGE | INCONCLUSIVE | NOT_RUN` — `PASS` is not representable.
  `economic_result` is `NOT_RUN` unless a caller supplies outcomes from a real economic run,
  and the validator refuses a decisive conclusion while it is `NOT_RUN`.
* CLI: `python -m v8_next.evaluation.coverage --tape <dir|tape.jsonl> [--json]`,
  with `--as-of`, `--role SYMBOL=ROLE`, `--default-role`, `--metadata`, `--out`.
  `SEALED_OOS` is not assignable from the CLI (no authorization is minted on this path).

Nothing is synthesized: missing bars, funding settlements, mark prices and metadata are
counted, named and published as `PARTIAL`/`BLOCKED`. There is no fallback tape and no
imputation path.

## 5. Explicit blockers and limits

| Item | Status | Prerequisite |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Altcoin PIT universe coverage | `NOT_RUN` | real altcoin tape (`M4-04:NO_ALTCOIN_TAPE_IN_REPO`) |
| Altcoin delisting history / survivorship exclusion | `BLOCKED` | venue metadata history per symbol |
| Mark-price coverage | `PARTIAL` | tape with a mark channel (absent in the in-repo tape) |
| Independent regime economic results | `NOT_RUN` | economic evaluation over altcoin windows |
| Sealed OOS re-evaluation | `NOT_RUN` | authorization-minted sealed window (not accessed here) |
| 24–48 h soak / live orders | `NOT_RUN` | out of scope; no orders and no credentials used |

No `PASS` outcome is produced anywhere in this work, and no quantity is extrapolated from the
BTC slice to the altcoin universe.

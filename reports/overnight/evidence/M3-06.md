# M3-06 — PIT altcoin-perpetual universe & 20 USDT order feasibility

## Objective

Build the research-plane rule engine that decides, for small-capital Binance USDT-M altcoin
perpetual research, (a) whether a contract may be evaluated at a point-in-time decision instant
and (b) whether a 20-USDT-class order can actually be expressed on it, with explicit
eligible/rejected statuses — and without projecting BTC results onto altcoin contracts.

## Scope and epistemic status

This repository contains **no real altcoin-perpetual tape**. The captured fixtures are BTC/ETH
class, and no listing/delisting/metadata history for altcoins exists on disk. Therefore:

- **Economic qualification of an altcoin universe is `NOT_RUN`** — it is an M4-04 dependency.
  Every `UniverseReport` carries `economic_qualification="NOT_RUN"` and
  `economic_blocker="M4-04:NO_ALTCOIN_TAPE_IN_REPO"` as structural fields (`Literal["NOT_RUN"]`,
  so the record cannot claim qualification). A test asserts this pin.
- The **rule engine itself is fully tested** against synthetic metadata fixtures in
  `tests/test_universe_feasibility.py`. No economic, performance or live-trading claim is made.
- No BTC (or any other) symbol's results are projected: metadata is per-symbol and every
  boundary/term comes from the supplied record; absent records are `BLOCKED`, never inferred.

## Implementation

New module `src/v8_next/adapters/universe.py` (987 lines, inert: no execution adapter import, no
venue order object, no I/O, no wall clock).

### Types

- `InstrumentMetadata` — PIT record: `symbol, venue, contract_type, listed_at,
  delisted_at|None, tick_size, step_size, min_notional, min_qty, quote_volume_24h,
  quote_volume_as_of_ns, leverage_max, maintenance_margin_rate, metadata_version, available_at`.
  Validated: positive finite Decimals, `delisted_at > listed_at`, `leverage_max >= 1`,
  `0 <= maintenance_margin_rate < 1`, volume and its snapshot time present together.
  `contract_digest` hashes **terms only** (exponent-normalized, so `0.010 == 0.01`) for change
  detection.
- `EligibilityStatus` — exactly the ten required statuses (`ELIGIBLE`, `NOT_LISTED`,
  `DELISTED`, `STALE_VOLUME`, `BELOW_MIN_NOTIONAL`, `CONTRACT_CHANGED`, `UNKNOWN_METADATA`,
  `INSUFFICIENT_EQUITY`, `SIZE_BELOW_MIN`, `EXCESSIVE_FRICTION`). A test pins the vocabulary.
- `UniverseReason` — machine-readable cause alongside the coarse status (no prose branching).
- `UniverseVerdict`, `FeasibilityVerdict`, `OrderSpec` (advisory-only, pinned), `MetadataResolution`,
  `CoverageSummary`, `UniverseEntry`, `UniverseReport`.

### `eligibility(metadata, *, as_of_ns, history=None, max_volume_age_ns=MAX_VOLUME_AGE_NS)`

Order of checks (first match wins, so a delisted contract reports `DELISTED` even when its
volume snapshot is also stale):

1. metadata `available_at > as_of_ns` → `UNKNOWN_METADATA` (never used, even with perfect volume);
2. non-`PERPETUAL` contract → `UNKNOWN_METADATA`;
3. `as_of_ns < listed_at` → `NOT_LISTED`; exactly at `listed_at` → evaluable;
4. `as_of_ns >= delisted_at` → `DELISTED` (`delisted_at - 1` is still trading);
5. missing volume snapshot → `UNKNOWN_METADATA`; snapshot timestamp in the future →
   `UNKNOWN_METADATA`; age > `MAX_VOLUME_AGE_NS` (documented research assumption: 1 h) →
   `STALE_VOLUME`; exactly at max age → `ELIGIBLE`;
6. otherwise `ELIGIBLE`.

Optional `history` establishes the as-of version via `resolve_metadata`: future records are
invisible — they are filtered out **before** the contradiction scan, so a not-yet-knowable record
can never change an as-of verdict; one `metadata_version` with two term sets *among the records
available at `as_of_ns`* is contradictory; two term sets sharing the newest availability stamp are
ambiguous → `CONTRACT_CHANGED`; a supplied version older than the resolved as-of version →
`CONTRACT_CHANGED` (`SUPERSEDED_METADATA_VERSION`). An announced future change is not applied
early.

### `feasibility(metadata, *, equity, target_notional, entry_price, fee_bps, slippage_bps, stop_distance_bps, leverage)`

Quantizes the price **down** to `tick_size` and quantity **down** to `step_size`, then rejects in
order: target below `min_notional` → `BELOW_MIN_NOTIONAL`; requested leverage above venue max and
equity·leverage below target → `INSUFFICIENT_EQUITY`; quantity rounds to zero or below `min_qty`
→ `SIZE_BELOW_MIN`; quantized notional below `min_notional` → `BELOW_MIN_NOTIONAL`; round-trip
fee+slippage (charged per side, twice) `>=` stop distance → `EXCESSIVE_FRICTION`; 1× margin `<=`
maintenance rate or stop at/through the isolated-margin liquidation distance
`(1/leverage - maintenance_margin_rate)` → `INSUFFICIENT_EQUITY`. On success it returns the
quantized `OrderSpec` (quantity, tick-aligned price, notional, initial margin, round-trip cost,
`advisory_only=True`). Invalid *inputs* (non-`Decimal`, non-finite, non-positive, price below one
tick) raise, they are not coerced.

### `qualify_universe(metadata_by_symbol, as_of_ns, **feasibility_kwargs) -> UniverseReport`

Accepts one record, a history, or `None` per symbol. `None` / empty history → `BLOCKED` with
`METADATA_ABSENT` / `NO_METADATA_IN_HISTORY` and no fabricated verdict; mapping key ≠ record
symbol → `BLOCKED` (`SYMBOL_MISMATCH`); unresolvable history → `BLOCKED` with the resolution
status. Empty mapping → report `NOT_RUN`. Without feasibility kwargs the report is
eligibility-only: eligible symbols stay `NOT_RUN` for coverage (`FEASIBILITY_NOT_RUN`) rather
than being called feasible on partial evidence. Coverage counts must sum to the entry count
(model invariant). Entries are sorted by symbol and report/verdict ids are
`canonical_digest` identities over the whole record, so input order does not change identity.

## Reuse boundary

`risk/admission.admit` and `adapters/native_tape.py` already encode the same quantization
semantics (floor to step, min quantity/notional) but only for live/live-sim paths over Nautilus
`CryptoPerpetual` objects. Those objects carry no as-of history, delisting state or availability
stamp, so the PIT record here is deliberately independent; the arithmetic semantics are aligned
with `admit` (floor-to-step, `BELOW_VENUE_MINIMUM`-style rejection) rather than reimplementing a
different convention.

## Repair (wave 23) — future records could poison the as-of verdict

Defect: `resolve_metadata` ran the `metadata_version -> term set` contradiction scan over **all**
records, including `available_at > as_of_ns`. One future record reusing the current version label
with different terms therefore flipped an as-of verdict from `ELIGIBLE` to
`CONTRACT_CHANGED` — a point-in-time leak by exactly the rule the module claims to enforce.

Fix: filter to `available_at <= as_of_ns` first, then scan contradictions over that usable set
(`src/v8_next/adapters/universe.py`, `resolve_metadata`). The ambiguity/`SUPERSEDED` rules were
already computed over the usable set and are unchanged.

Tests added (`tests/test_universe_feasibility.py`):

- `test_future_record_reusing_the_version_label_cannot_flip_an_as_of_verdict` — the audit's exact
  reproduction (`as_of = T0 + 10`, future record at `T0 + 110` reusing `v1` with a different tick
  size) now yields `ELIGIBLE` and `ResolutionStatus.RESOLVED` on the available record. Fails before
  the fix with `CONTRACT_CHANGED`.
- `test_contradiction_scan_is_bounded_by_the_as_of_instant` — a conflicting record available
  exactly at `as_of_ns` still yields `CONTRACT_CHANGED`/`CONTRADICTORY_METADATA_VERSION`, while the
  same record one nanosecond later is future evidence and leaves the verdict `ELIGIBLE`. Fails
  before the fix on the second half.

## Residual risks

- **Economic qualification is `NOT_RUN`** (no altcoin tape in-repo; M4-04 dependency). No claim is
  made about real altcoin liquidity, spreads, funding, or capacity.
- `MAX_VOLUME_AGE_NS = 1 h` is a documented staleness assumption, not a measured value.
- Liquidation distance is the isolated-margin approximation `1/leverage - maintenance_margin_rate`;
  funding, mark-price basis, fee-adjusted liquidation and cross-margin are not modelled.
- Friction is a fixed bps round trip; no depth/impact model exists without a tape.
- `min_quantity`/`max_quantity` above 0 are not modelled (`max_qty` is absent from the record);
  requests are constrained by `target_notional` and `equity`.
- The Binance exchangeInfo wire decoding remains in `adapters/native_tape.py`; this module takes
  already-decoded PIT records, so no live metadata fetch is performed here.

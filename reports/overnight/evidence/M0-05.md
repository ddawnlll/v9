# M0-05 — Native bracket, reduce-only, partial-fill and protection invariants

**Issue:** #6 · **Worker:** `NativeProtection` · **Branch:** `wave3/w3h`
**Base commit:** `a6b569765903745a5d09ee71f047feb8f13814e8` (`integration/wave1`)
**Implementation commit:** `284a8e9c2611709a2c94775a1e0636391c0addfe`

## Objective

Close the remaining native execution-safety gaps with real position/order identities,
without introducing a second OMS: qualify that a bracket's protective quantity tracks
the actual filled position, that reduce-only orders cannot reverse, that a rejected
cancel cannot silently drop protection, that stop/target races and restarts replay
deterministically, and that rejected/unknown/gapped protection can never be read as
`PROTECTED`.

## What was implemented

New module `src/v8_next/adapters/protection_invariants.py` — pure, native-agnostic,
side-effect-free. It imports no execution library and constructs/cancels nothing.

- `ProtectionState`: `PROTECTED | UNPROTECTED | PARTIAL | REJECTED | UNKNOWN | GAP`.
- `ProtectionEvent`: frozen, validated, keyed by native `position_id` /
  `client_order_id`. Kinds: `ORDER_SUBMITTED | ACK | PARTIAL_FILL | FILL |
  CANCEL_REQUEST | CANCELED | REJECTED | POSITION_CHANGED`. Unknown enum values,
  floats, negative quantities and fills without a cumulative quantity raise
  `ValueError` (fail closed, never defaulted).
- `BracketInvariantChecker`: pure reducer exposing `state`, `state_of(position_id)`,
  `position_quantity`, `protective_quantity` (live acked stop leg), `target_quantity`,
  `order_remaining`, `violations`, `snapshot()` and a canonical `digest()`.
- Invariants:
  1. The live, acknowledged stop quantity must equal the filled position quantity. A
     partial entry fill leaves the incumbent leg under-sized (`PARTIAL` +
     `PROTECTION_QUANTITY_MISMATCH`) until an `ACK` restates the amended quantity.
  2. Reduce-only remaining quantity above the open position is
     `REDUCE_ONLY_OVER_QUANTITY`; a fill beyond the open position is clamped at flat and
     reported (`REDUCE_ONLY_REVERSAL_BLOCKED` / `PROTECTIVE_FILL_AFTER_FLAT`) — the
     position never flips sign.
  3. Live stop/target must oppose the position side and be ordered correctly
     (`stop < target` long, `stop > target` short); otherwise `UNKNOWN`.
  4. `CANCEL_REQUEST` never removes protection; `REJECTED` with `cancel_reject=True`
     leaves the order live (`CANCEL_REJECT_ORDER_STILL_LIVE`, state stays `PROTECTED`).
  5. Rejected, unknown, unacknowledged (`GAP`), unverifiable or over-sized protection
     cannot yield `PROTECTED`.
  6. Protection that survives its position's close is marked stale and can never cover a
     new — or reopened — position id; a fill belonging to stale protection is
     `STALE_FILL_IGNORED` and is never applied to the current position.
- Replay: event identity is the canonical event payload, so re-applying a journal is a
  no-op (restart idempotence). Backwards cumulative fills are `STALE_FILL_IGNORED` and
  never applied; unknown references are `UNKNOWN_ORDER_REFERENCE`; snapshot conflicts
  are `POSITION_QUANTITY_CONFLICT`.

## Residual risks / limits

- The checker reasons over the event contract, not the venue. A venue/adapter that
  amends protective quantity without re-emitting an `ACK` with the new total quantity
  will read `PARTIAL` (deliberately conservative) until the resize is observable.
- Sibling cancellation is not inferred: only `CANCELED` retires a leg; a second leg that
  reports a fill after flat is named (`PROTECTIVE_FILL_AFTER_FLAT`), never absorbed.
- Protection is qualified per native `position_id`; `state` reports the most recently
  opened position. Concurrent positions are each queryable via `state_of`.
- Event identity deduplicates exact payloads: a byte-identical re-send is treated as a
  replay (intended for restart), not as a second economic event.
- A position snapshot is authoritative; a snapshot that arrives before the fills it
  summarizes is adopted and can overstate until the next snapshot/conflict record.
- The checker has no opinion on price levels beyond stop/target ordering; it does not
  validate stop distance or risk sizing (other modules own those).
